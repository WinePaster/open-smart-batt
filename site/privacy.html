總則

OpenSmartBatt 不蒐集、不儲存、不傳輸任何個人資料。本 App 為離線工具，所有資料只留在您自己的裝置上。

App 的資料處理

App 產生的資料（已儲存裝置、遙測歷史、設定、診斷日誌，以及您開啟時的速度與 G 值）皆僅儲存在您裝置本機的資料庫，不會上傳到任何伺服器，我們也無法存取。

藍牙（Bluetooth）僅用於與您自己的電池／電容硬體通訊，不用於定位、不用於追蹤，也不蒐集廣告識別碼。

定位（選用，預設關閉）：只有在您開啟「速度偵測」時才會要求定位權限，且只在儀表板畫面、App 位於前景時使用 GPS，退到背景就停止。App 只從 GPS 取得速度：連線期間的速度會寫入本機紀錄，並包含在您匯出的診斷檔裡；位置座標永遠不會被儲存，也不會出現在任何匯出檔中。

加速度計（選用，預設關閉）：只有在您開啟 G 值錶時才會讀取，且只在儀表板顯示期間讀取。前後與橫向 G 值會寫入本機紀錄，並包含在您匯出的診斷檔裡。

通知：警告通知完全由手機本機產生，不經任何推播伺服器。

匯出與分享：CSV 歷史與診斷日誌只有在您自己按下匯出／分享時才會離開手機，要送給誰由您在系統分享選單中決定。匯出檔內含裝置的藍牙識別資訊、遙測歷史，以及您開啟過的速度與 G 值。

檢查更新：僅 GitHub 下載的 Android 版會向 GitHub 檢查新版本（只讀取公開的 release 資訊，不傳送可識別您身分的資料）。App Store 與 Google Play 版本由商店負責更新，不連線 GitHub。

本 App 不含任何第三方分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK。

本網站

本網站為靜態頁面，不設任何表單、不蒐集 email、不使用追蹤 Cookie。iOS 的 TestFlight／App Store 與 Google Play 下載分別透過 Apple 與 Google 的服務完成，適用各自的隱私政策（ Apple、Google）；我們不經手您的 email 或帳號資料。

您的權利

由於我們未持有您的任何個人資料，無資料可供查詢、更正或刪除。若要移除 App 產生的本機資料，於裝置上解除安裝 App 即可。

聯絡

有隱私或授權相關問題，請至 GitHub Issues 提出。

Summary

OpenSmartBatt collects, stores, and transmits no personal data. The app is an offline tool; all data stays on your own device.

Data handling in the app

Data the app creates (saved devices, telemetry history, settings, diagnostic log, and speed and G values when you enable them) is stored only in a local database on your device. It is never uploaded to any server and we cannot access it.

Bluetooth is used solely to communicate with your own battery/capacitor hardware — not for location, not for tracking, and no advertising identifiers are collected.

Location (optional, off by default): location permission is requested only when you turn on speed detection, and GPS is used only on the dashboard while the app is in the foreground; it stops when the app is backgrounded. The app takes only speed from GPS: speed recorded while connected is written to the local history and is included in the diagnostic files you export. Location coordinates are never stored and never appear in any export.

Accelerometer (optional, off by default): read only when you turn on the G-meter, and only while the dashboard is on screen. Longitudinal and lateral G values are written to the local history and are included in the diagnostic files you export.

Notifications: alerts are generated entirely on your phone; no push server is involved.

Export and sharing: CSV history and diagnostic logs leave your phone only when you export or share them yourself, to whoever you pick in the system share sheet. Exports contain your devices' Bluetooth identifiers, telemetry history, and any speed and G values you enabled.

Update check: only the Android build downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub for a newer release (reading public release info only; nothing that identifies you is sent). App Store and Google Play builds are updated by the store and never contact GitHub.

The app contains no third-party analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs.

This website

This site is static: no forms, no email collection, no tracking cookies. Downloads through TestFlight / the App Store and Google Play are handled by Apple and Google under their own privacy policies (Apple, Google); we never handle your email or account data.

Your rights

Because we hold none of your personal data, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our side. To remove the app's local data, uninstall the app from your device.

Contact

For privacy or licensing questions, please open a GitHub issue.
